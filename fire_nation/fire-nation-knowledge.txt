=== SECTION: stat_guidance_strength ===
ALIASES: strength, str, stat build, breakpoints, optimal stats, build guide, how much str
The goal is to get as close to maximum overall damage/performance as possible while spending as few stat points in Strength as possible. Use the Fire model data to find efficient damage, speed, range, and duration breakpoints. Don't just max STR automatically.

When recommending Strength, start by considering these two key Fire STR values:
- 378 effective STR — the primary efficient Fire breakpoint. This should generally be the first recommendation for players who want strong Fire performance while preserving as many stat points as possible for Defence/Stamina.
- 423 effective STR — the primary high-offence Fire breakpoint. This should generally be the first recommendation for players who want to push their Fire damage/performance further while still avoiding unnecessary Strength beyond the important 423 breakpoint.

These are starting points, not mandatory values. Use the player's goals to decide which is more appropriate:
- If they prioritise stat efficiency, survivability, or Stamina, favour 378 STR.
- If they prioritise damage/offence and are willing to spend more points on Strength, favour 423 STR.
- If they want a balanced build, compare 378 and 423 and explain the trade-off.
- If they specifically want maximum Fire performance, consider STR values above 423 if the Fire model data shows meaningful additional benefits.
- If they have limited available stat points or substantial STR from gear, recommend a lower allocated STR value when their effective STR can still reach an important breakpoint.
- If their playstyle or build benefits from a different breakpoint, recommend another STR value when the Fire model data supports it.

Always compare the marginal benefit of moving from one breakpoint to another. Explain what additional damage, speed, range, or duration the player gains for the extra Strength.

Do not recommend 400 STR simply because it is a round number, and do not automatically recommend the highest possible STR.
=== END SECTION ===

=== SECTION: stat_guidance_defence ===
ALIASES: defence, defense, hp regen, health regen, def
Ask whether they want 4 HP regen or 5 HP regen.
- 4 HP regen = 350 HP in the bar
- 5 HP regen = 450 HP in the bar
- Players start with 100 HP by default, so without gear:
  - 4 HP regen requires 250 Defence
  - 5 HP regen requires 350 Defence

Gear Defence counts toward this. For example, if someone needs 250 effective DEF but their gear gives +40 DEF, they only need 210 allocated DEF.
=== END SECTION ===

=== SECTION: stat_guidance_stamina ===
ALIASES: stamina, sta, raid build, 1v1 build, tanky build
This depends mainly on the type of build.
- Raid builds: usually favour 4 HP regen with more Stamina.
- 1v1 builds: can use either 4 HP or 5 HP regen.
- Tanky 1v1 builds: can favour 5 HP regen with lower Stamina.
- After finding efficient STR and the desired DEF threshold, Stamina will generally receive the remaining available points.
=== END SECTION ===

=== SECTION: stat_guidance_gear ===
ALIASES: gear, equipment, effective stats, allocated stats, gear bonuses
Gear is extremely important. Before recommending final stats, always ask:
1. Do you have gear?
2. What gear are you using?
3. How much STR, DEF and/or STA does each piece give you?

Account for all gear bonuses when calculating the build. Gear makes stat allocation more flexible because the player can reach important effective-stat thresholds without spending as many of their natural stat points.

Always distinguish between allocated stats and effective stats after gear. For example, 378 effective STR could be:
- 378 allocated + 0 gear STR
- 350 allocated + 28 gear STR
- 330 allocated + 48 gear STR

These should all be treated as 378 effective STR for Fire performance.

The overall goal is not simply to make the numbers as high as possible. The goal is to create the most stat-efficient build for that specific player and their playstyle, using 378 STR and 423 STR as the two primary Fire benchmarks and selecting other Strength values when the player's goals, gear, or available stat points make them more appropriate.
=== END SECTION ===
